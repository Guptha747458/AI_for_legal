FREELANCE SERVICES AGREEMENT (REVISED)

1. Services. The Contractor shall provide website design, development, and maintenance services as described in Exhibit A.

2. Term. This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue for a term of two (2) years, unless earlier terminated as provided herein. This Agreement shall automatically renew for successive one-year terms unless either party provides sixty (60) days' written notice before renewal.

3. Payment. Client shall pay Contractor $6,000 per month within thirty (30) days of invoice. Late payments shall bear interest at 2.0% per month plus a $100 late fee.

4. Termination. Either party may terminate this Agreement upon sixty (60) days' written notice to the other party. Upon termination, Client shall pay for all services rendered plus a $1,000 early-termination fee.

5. Indemnification. Each party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party from claims arising out of its own negligence or misconduct.

6. Liability. Neither party shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages. Contractor's total liability shall not exceed the fees paid in the prior twelve (12) months.

7. Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising under this Agreement shall be resolved by binding arbitration in New York, New York, and each party waives the right to a jury trial.

8. Confidentiality. Each party agrees to keep confidential all non-public information received from the other party for five (5) years after disclosure.

9. Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of New York.

10. Notices. All notices must be sent in writing with fourteen (14) days' advance notice to be effective.
